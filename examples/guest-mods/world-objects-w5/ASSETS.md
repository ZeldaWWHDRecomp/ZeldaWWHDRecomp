# Original several-material island assets

The island, its six textures and its collision were generated by
`assets/make_fixtures.py` for this example, MPL-2.0. The script reproduces
`assets/island.glb` using only Python's standard library. No game models,
textures, collision files or archives are included.

| Material | Kind | Texture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sand` | opaque | 128 × 128, repeating |
| `grass` | opaque | 128 × 128, repeating |
| `rock` | opaque | 128 × 128, repeating |
| `wood` | opaque | 128 × 128, planks |
| `fence` | cut-out (`MASK`), double-sided | 128 × 128, posts and rails over transparency |
| `bush` | cut-out (`MASK`), double-sided | 128 × 128, leaves over transparency |

The visible mesh has six primitives, one per material: 208 triangles (228
drawn, as the double-sided cut-outs get a back copy), 624 vertices and 384 KiB
of decoded textures. A separate `island_col` mesh supplies
sand, grass, stone and wood collision from the walkable surfaces; the fences and
bushes are not solid. The coast extends below the sea for the native ship
collision samples.

`python3 assets/make_fixtures.py --atlas OUT.glb` writes the same island with
one material and one 256 × 256 texture atlas, without the cut-outs, for
comparing the single-material conversion.

The example is placed at (75000,0,-201500), sea room 12, the same area as the
W1, W3 and W4 examples; enable it on its own.
