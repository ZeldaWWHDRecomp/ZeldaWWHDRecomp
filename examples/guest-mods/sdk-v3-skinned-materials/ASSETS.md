# Original reed walker

`assets/reed_walker.glb` is generated by `assets/make_reed_walker.py` for this
example, MPL-2.0. The geometric mesh, seven-bone rig, three painted textures and
the `idle` and `walk` clips are original; the script uses only Python's standard
library and writes the same bytes on every platform. No game models, textures,
shaders, materials or animations are included.

| Material | Kind | Texture | Parts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `cloth` | opaque | 128 × 128, blue and white stripes | lower body, legs |
| `bark` | opaque | 128 × 128, orange grain with a face | upper body, head |
| `leaf` | cut-out (`MASK`), double-sided | 128 × 128, a leaf over transparency | crest, leaf arms |

The body is one tube cut into the `cloth` and `bark` materials at the waist. The
ring of vertices where they meet is in both primitives with the same weights
(half hips, half body), so the waist stays closed when the `idle` clip bends the
body forward and back. The `walk` clip swings the legs and leaf arms. The model
has 258 vertices and 300 triangles (308 drawn, as the double-sided leaves get a
back copy) and 192 KiB of decoded textures. Units are metres, positive Y is up and
the face looks along positive Z; the bones have identity bind rotations.

The walker follows a short route on the Windfall pier (sea room 11). Talking to
it plays a demo step in which it walks towards the camera with its `walk` clip and
then plays `idle`. The example requires Story Notebook.
