# Original sky island assets

`assets/island.glb` (the floating island: its grass top, rocky underside and stone path) and `assets/arena.glb`
(the walled boss arena north of it) were generated by `assets/make_island.py` for this example, MPL-2.0; the
script reproduces them without external assets. The models carry no texture: the game draws them with
materials of the first room of the Tower of the Gods' outside stage, taken from the player's own game files when
the stage is built, under that stage's sky. No game models, textures, collision files or archives are included.

`assets/sky.wwht` is the stage's shader template, made with `tools/shaderprep.py stage-template` after playing
the stage: a code-free list of shader hashes and render settings (it passes the release guard's check). It
holds no shader code and nothing of the game's files; setup prepares the shaders from the player's own files.
