# Original mod dungeon assets

`assets/hall.glb` and `assets/vault.glb` (floors, walls and ceilings of the dungeon's own two rooms) were
generated by `assets/make_rooms.py` for this example, MPL-2.0; the script reproduces them without external
assets. The models carry no texture: the game draws them with materials of the lava cave's first room, and the
dungeon's middle room is the lava cave's second room as it is, both taken from the player's own game files
when the stage is built. No game models, textures, collision files or archives are included.
