# Surround speakers

In **Settings → Audio → Speakers**, choose **Surround 5.1** and restart the game.
**Stereo** is the default. Use your normal save or a portable save state after
switching speakers; full debug save states require the speaker setting they were
created with (older full states require Stereo).

Surround sends six separate PCM channels to the selected system output. The device
must already be configured for multichannel playback. If six channels are unavailable,
the game uses Stereo and shows a note in Audio settings. If the output changes during
surround playback, audio is muted until you restart the game to negotiate that output.

## Check your speakers in one minute

1. Select Surround 5.1, restart, and confirm **Current output: Surround 5.1**.
2. Press **Test speakers**. Over six seconds, the screen names each speaker:
   Front left, Front right, Centre, Subwoofer, Surround left, Surround right.
   Each should sound only from the named speaker. The subwoofer tone is lower
   pitched and may be quieter. **Stop speaker test** ends the sequence.
3. Play outside on Dragon Roost Island, near the sea, and turn the camera around
   environmental sounds. The rainy shoreline is a useful comparison. Compare Stereo and Surround after restarting. Music and ambience need
   not use every speaker continuously; a silent LFE channel in a scene is normal.
4. Press Minus for Off-TV Play and return to TV play. GamePad audio should remain
   audible through the front speakers. Check any audio mods and your usual fast
   forward setting too.

When reporting a mapping problem, include your operating system, audio device,
current output shown in Settings, and which physical speaker sounded for each name.
No game-audio recording is needed.

Stereo audio mods and GamePad sound use front left/right. Volume and fast-forward
settings apply to all six channels. The speaker test temporarily replaces game audio
and plays at a low level; system bass management may also route low frequencies from
other speakers to the subwoofer.
